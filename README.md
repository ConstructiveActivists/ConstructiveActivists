Constructive Activist is APILA Capital's research and tech venture incubator and accelerator. The platform serves as a living laboratory for technological experimentation and rigorous research, exploring how deep technology can be applied to unlock trapped value by combining individual talent with teamwork.

Our foundation is first-principles thinking.
Inspired by our collaboration with the MIT Center for Collective Intelligence, which demonstrated the true power of human-machine synergy, we break complexity down into manageable variables and reconstruct effective solutions by leveraging tech.

As a proof of concept, we tested our frameworks and workflows on Venezuela's debt default, which led to The Venezuela White Paper think tank initiative.
